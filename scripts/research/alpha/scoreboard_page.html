      Can a simple rule beat just holding the US market?

      Tested on 61 years of data (1963-2024), rules fixed in advance, judged on 2013-24. No rule beat holding. The trend rule is the only one with a use: it cut the 2000-12 fall from 55% to 22%, and it cost about 2.5 points a year in 2013-24 when markets rose.
